How we decide what is "normal"
  • Weeks run Monday to Sunday in the account's timezone.
  • Each location is compared with its own 8 previous full weeks. A week with no activity counts as 0.
  • Normal range = the middle value of those weeks, plus or minus the usual week-to-week swing.
  • With low volumes we compare against the range, not a percentage.
